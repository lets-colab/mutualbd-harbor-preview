01 Since 1962

Across quiet waters, global trade moves in stillness.

A family business on the port city's waterfront.

Al-Hajj Swaleh Ahmed founded Sasco Overseas as a trading company in 1962. After 1998 his sons, Rais Ahmed and Parvez Ahmed, founded Mutual Group in Chittagong, which has handled customs clearance and shipping since 1992.

02 Arrival · Mutual Shipping Ltd.

Night meets the horizon. Every glowing light marks a promise in transit.

The agent meets the ship.

An international shipping agent for bulk, general, project and specialised cargo, working inside the customs-restricted area of Chittagong Port.

03 Berth · Pancharagh Udayon Shangstha Ltd.

Where land meets tide, heavy cargo rests under watchful floodlights.

Alongside at Berth No. 8.

Stevedore and berth operator at Berth No. 8, Chittagong Port Authority, since 2003: bulk, general and project cargo, and machinery.

04 Clearance · Mutual Trading Ltd.

Through quiet corridors of steel, trade finds its path.

Cleared and released.

Customs clearing and forwarding since 1992: food grain in bulk, steel, logs, and containerised cargo for ceramics, raw materials, garments and textiles.

05 River · Central Navigation Ltd.

Upward along calm, misted waters, the river carries trade inland.

By river to the inland jetties.

Owner and operator of inland coastal vessels, lightering cargo to shallow-draft river jetties across the country.

06 Road · Modern Logistics

From harbour lights to the open highway.

The last miles to the factory.

Heavy equipment and container transport, and inland coastal vessel supply, arranged for importers and exporters.

07 Beyond the port · M Tech · Sun Valley Developments Ltd.

Where trade builds cities. This is the pulse of Chittagong.

What the cargo builds.

M Tech supplies engineering and safety systems: CCTV and access control, electrical, fire safety, automation, petrochemical solutions and thermographic testing. Sun Valley Developments is the group's land and real estate company.
