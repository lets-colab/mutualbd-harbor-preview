Mutual Group

A Chittagong family business since 1962

Al-Hajj Swaleh Ahmed founded Sasco Overseas in 1962. Customs clearance and shipping followed in 1992, and since 1998 his sons, Rais Ahmed and Parvez Ahmed, have led the group.
